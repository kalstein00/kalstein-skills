## What it does

Establish the observed symptom and supported cause without authorizing production repair. The existing diagnosis loop supplies falsifiable probes and evidence.

## When to reach for it

Invoke this command explicitly when you want the cause established before deciding on a fix.

## Prerequisites

The target project and the inputs/authority required by the owning task. Preserve its existing setup and documentation.

## Common questions

**Will asking for diagnosis change production behavior?**

No. A separately authorized fix enters the applicable implementation route.

## It's working if

- The report separates confirmed cause from unverified hypotheses.
- It names the reproduction and the observations that reject material alternatives.
